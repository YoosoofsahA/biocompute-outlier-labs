Why we trust it
Rebound
Used to banana? Show fresh strawberry. The response comes straight back: it's detecting change, not tiredness.
Same drive
Every image gets exactly 12 pulses, logged on every frame. More response never means more stimulation.
Adaptation off
Turn adaptation off and the dish can't tell fresh from spoiled. The effect comes from habituation.
